# NIRN.Ai - Example Queries for the LLM

This document provides a guide on the types of questions and drafting requests you can ask the NIRN.Ai assistant. Since the corpus contains thousands of Government Resolutions (GRs), querying by specific departments or policy areas yields the best results.

## 🏢 Common / General Questions (All Departments)

These are questions that apply universally across government operations, administrative rules, and state-level policies:

- **Leave & Attendance:** "What are the latest rules regarding maternity leave for state government employees?"
- **Transfers:** "Show me the GRs related to the general transfer policy of Class 1 and Class 2 officers."
- **Allowances:** "What is the current Dearness Allowance (DA) percentage for state government employees as of the latest GR?"
- **Procurement & Tendering:** "What are the financial limits for e-tendering for government purchases?"
- **Holidays:** "Provide the list of public holidays declared by the state government for the current year."
- **Drafting Assistance:** "Help me draft a GR to form a 5-member committee to review office timings."
- **Conflict Checking:** "I want to propose a new policy giving 10 extra casual leaves. Check if this conflicts with existing GAD regulations."

## 🎓 Higher and Technical Education Department
*This is the primary focus department of the NIRN.Ai system.*

- "What is the fee structure for lateral entry admissions in engineering colleges?"
- "Show me the GR regarding the delayed enrolment procedure for technical institutions."
- "What are the eligibility criteria for the Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti scheme?"
- "Provide the guidelines for starting a new private pharmacy college in the state."
- "What is the grant-in-aid formula for non-agricultural universities?"
- **Drafting:** "Draft a new GR outlining the revised PhD stipend amounts for research scholars in state universities."

### 💡 Pro-Tips for Prompting the LLM:
1. **Be Specific:** Instead of asking "Show me education GRs", ask "Show me the GRs regarding scholarship disbursement delays in higher education."
2. **Use Years/Dates:** If you know the rough timeline, include it: "What was the DA hike GR issued in 2023?"
3. **Ask for Comparisons:** "Compare the 2018 and 2022 GRs regarding the transfer policy."
4. **Drafting Constraints:** When drafting, provide constraints: "Draft a GR for a new committee. It must include 1 Chairman from Finance and 2 members from GAD. Format it in formal Marathi/English."
